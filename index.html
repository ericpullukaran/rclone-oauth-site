Eric's personal Drive sync

A personal installation of rclone that synchronizes selected files between my Google Drive account and my own computer.

What this app does

This Google OAuth client identifies my rclone installation when I sign in to Google and authorize file synchronization. The setup maintains a local copy of a selected notes folder and synchronizes changes in both directions.

Personal use

This installation is operated solely for my own use. It is not offered as a public service and this website provides no sign-up or login feature.

Data and access

The client requests Google Drive access to read, create, update, and delete files as needed for synchronization. Although the Google authorization permits broad Drive access, the configured sync job operates on a selected folder. Files are transferred between Google Drive and my computer.

Read the privacy policy for details about permissions, storage, and revoking access.
